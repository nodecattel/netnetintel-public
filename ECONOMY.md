Both weekly-points and RWA allocations are spread over four weeks: 50% / 25% / 15% / 10%. Each purchase creates another overlapping schedule, independent of promotional dates. Bounty, supporter, buyback, reserve and operations allocations are not four-week player-pot schedules. Native tokens are accounted separately; USDG and wsNET balances must not be added as if they shared decimals or price.

Governance can change future policy and freeze it. A new percentage does not reprice existing earmarks or settled claims. Inspect live policy before relying on the illustrative defaults above.

The treasury reserve starts from 3% of sales. Target planning is four to eight weeks of top-ups, not a guaranteed minimum pot. Player claims and supporter principal remain separate. Count zero future buyback recovery or staking income until actually received and available. Supporter capacity should use recurring sales, excluding a launch spike.
