# Supporter economics and limits

Private main revision: `7a91dbe5ce0e7d5766afe4ac9991bd51061c8768` · committed 2026-10-27T11:26:25+07:00.

Documentation only. No Solidity, application, backend or calculator source is published here. This is not an audit or a claim of explorer source verification. Live balances and current configuration: [Under the Hood](https://netnetintel.com/under-the-hood) and [Funded Pots](https://netnetintel.com/turfwar-funding).

Sales allocate 5% to the USDG supporter route and 5% to the wsNET supporter route under the initial policy. These are separate token accounting systems. They are not fixed deposit yields.

The pilot's weekly top-up exposure is capped at 2% of opening capital. Underwriting top-ups can reduce capital. Costs are constrained by received fees. Reserve funds, player liabilities and supporter principal must not be treated as interchangeable. Operator settlement, review and withdrawal timing remain material dependencies.

Deposits and capacity are gated; a ready policy or deployed vault is not an open deposit offer. Read the live status. Budget using recurring sales rather than annualizing launch revenue. No assumed staking or buyback profit subsidizes a forecast.
